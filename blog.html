NoteZ Enters Its Next Chapter with NicoZ Pro

On December 3, 2026, NoteZ moves beyond its Beta stage with a more polished experience, enhanced AI capabilities, and new ways to work and connect.

December 3, 2026 • NICO.world

NoteZ is entering its next chapter. After the Beta stage, we are moving forward with a more polished and capable experience designed around everyday digital work and productivity.

Introducing NicoZ Pro

NicoZ Pro launches on December 3, 2026 at ₹50/month.

The core NoteZ experience remains available for free, while Pro introduces additional capabilities for users who want a more complete experience.

Supported generated content can also be created without NICO branding with the Pro experience.

More Capable AI Summary

AI Summary is being improved into a more polished and reliable experience, with support for longer documents and more useful AI-generated summaries.

Introducing THE-CONECT

THE-CONECT is a new experience focused on connecting one person with another person and AI.

It represents a new direction for how people can communicate and interact with AI inside the NoteZ ecosystem.

Your Personal Workplace

Personal Workplace brings everyday digital tools together into one personal environment.

Work editor, calculator, time, planner, calendar, AI, personal activities, and other tools can come together as a digital space designed around the user.

Users can also choose information they want their AI to remember, allowing future conversations to have more useful context.

“December 3 marks more than a new release. It marks the next chapter of NoteZ.”

What's Next

NicoZ Pro is only the beginning. NICO.world is continuing to develop new tools, AI capabilities, and digital experiences for NoteZ.

We are building toward a future where the tools people use every day can feel more connected, personal, and useful.
